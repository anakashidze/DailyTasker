# TaskNext 1.2.1 — Task-list interaction fixes

- Fixed the idle SwipeToDismiss background so "Delete" is not permanently drawn underneath translucent task rows.
- Removed the pencil/Edit icon from task rows.
- Tapping only the center content area opens the task editor; the drag handle and Bonus star stay independent.
- Task titles are constrained to two lines with ellipsis and cannot overlap status pills or action icons.
- Dragging now gives haptic feedback on grab.
- Swipe action backgrounds are hidden while dragging, removing the Delete/Complete silhouette under a dragged task.
- Backlog items animate into their new positions when reordered.
- Dragged rows lift slightly and follow the finger while neighboring rows animate, making swaps easier to understand.
- Version bumped to 1.2.1 (versionCode 5).
